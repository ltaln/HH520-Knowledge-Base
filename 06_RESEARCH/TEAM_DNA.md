# Team DNA

## Purpose

Capture team-specific structural tendencies that may help interpret:
- attack
- defense
- recent form
- matchup consistency
- upset risk

## Current status

Team DNA exists as a research framework and may be passed as supporting evidence when available.

It is not currently allowed to receive a fixed high Stable weight merely because the field exists.

## Production behavior

The prediction builder may require supporting team structure for fine-grained predictions.
If evidence is missing, the system may PASS rather than fabricate certainty.

## Promotion standard

Any future Team DNA rule should:
1. use pre-match-only data
2. have a clear definition
3. meet sample/date thresholds
4. survive later-window validation
5. avoid team identity leakage / post-match contamination
6. remain explainable
