League DNA is research context, not a hard-coded production weight by default.

## Shadow caution

Some league-specific rules were held because September sample sizes were too small or performance fell below baseline.

Examples:
- Norway and Portugal league rules: insufficient Shadow sample

## Rule

League DNA can inform research and risk interpretation.
It must not become a permanent Stable weight without sufficient multi-window evidence.
